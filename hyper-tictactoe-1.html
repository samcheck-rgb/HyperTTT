How to play

  • The big 3×3 grid contains nine small 3×3 boards.
  • Wherever you play inside a small board decides where your opponent must play next — e.g. playing top-right sends them to the top-right board.
  • If that target board is already won or full, your opponent may play anywhere.
  • First small board: the first player to win any small board wins the game.
  • Three in a row: win three small boards in a row on the big board.
  • Memory mode: only the board you must play on is visible. Each player can peek at every board twice per game.
  • Play vs AI: an AI Gateway model studies the board and picks a legal move. Harder levels use stronger models and more tactical analysis. You can change difficulty at any time.
